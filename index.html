Chat phrasebook

Every local chat trick, in the order the game checks them. Online matches ignore all of them.

  • I like nuking people3D tactical nuke. Every enemy piece dies, their king survives, you keep the move.
  • Double troubleClones every non-king piece for both sides onto the nearest free square. Also: double the pieces, clone army.
  • Knight rushAll pawns become knights. Also: knight party.
  • Promotion partyAll pawns become queens. Also: queen me.
  • Flip itSpins the board 180 degrees in 3D. Position unchanged. Also: flip the board.
  • I winInstant checkmate for whoever typed it. Also: checkmate please, instant win.
  • GravityEvery piece falls to the bottom of its file, stacking by rank. Dropping both armies into the same files can end the game, which is half the fun.
  • Shuffle upEvery rook, knight, bishop and queen is dealt a random square. Pawns and kings stay put. Also: scramble, shuffle.
  • Identity crisisEvery piece changes colour, kings included. Also: swap colours, swap colors, invert.
  • RewindUndoes every move in the game back to the start. Also: take it back, undo everything.
  • TeleportThe two kings trade squares through portals. Also: king swap, beam me up.
